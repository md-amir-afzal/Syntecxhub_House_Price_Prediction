# MediCare AI product plan

## India-first principles
1. Voice-first, not form-first.
2. Hindi, English and Hinglish from day one.
3. Large touch targets and readable typography for elderly users.
4. Low-bandwidth, minimal images and small payloads.
5. Emergency safety takes priority over conversational UX.
6. Never store sensitive health data unless necessary and consented.

## Production roadmap
- Add real speech-to-text with explicit language detection.
- Add authenticated per-user encrypted assessment history.
- Add doctor/health-facility search with verified providers.
- Replace demo analyzer with validated clinical decision support logic reviewed by clinicians.
- Add monitoring, audit logs and abuse/risk controls.
- Conduct medical, privacy, security and accessibility review before clinical use.
